# 800x600 on the Fruit Jam

`-DUBIQOS_VIDEO_MODE=800x600` (with `UBIQOS_VIDEO=chargen` and
`UBIQOS_SYS_MHZ=240`) drives HDMI at VESA 800x600@60: 1056 x 628 at a
40 MHz pixel clock, both syncs positive. The default is still 640x480.

## Status, 22 Nov 2026

Seen on a monitor: a Raspberry Pi Monitor shows it sharp and stable, with
WiFi, SD and the USB host all running. The signal was right by every bench
measure already (the frame table read back over the probe adds up to
1056 x 628 pixel periods, the beam counter gives 60.31 frames a second
against VESA's 60.32, both PLLs read back where they were set), and the
character generator keeps up with no underruns at about 16 % of the
processor.

The receiver that did not show it is a MacroSilicon MS2130 capture dongle
(USB 345F:2130): it showed 800x600 once in many attempts, though it shows
640x480 every time. Do not judge a video mode by that dongle. QuickTime's
preview makes it worse: it can stay black after the input changes mode
until QuickTime is quit and opened again.

## What it does

- **Text**: 100 x 36 cells of 8x16. 600 lines is not a multiple of 16 and
  the 48 scanline buffers must divide the text height, so the text is 576
  lines and the 12 lines above and below show one blank line of their own.
- **Frame table**: 2 x 600 + 28 entries, padded to 2048 for the DMA ring by
  cutting each blank line into about thirty timed pieces of idle. Padding
  with single NOP transfers gave no picture at all: a NOP takes no time on
  the wire, the eight-word HSTX FIFO drains, and the output stalls once a
  frame.
- **Pump**: every 400 us instead of 700. At 700 the beam gained 33 lines a
  pump on a ring of 46 and the generator underran about 200 lines a second.
- **Clocks** (kernel/main.c): 40 MHz needs 200 MHz into HSTX, which 240
  does not divide to. USB and the ADC move to the system PLL (240 / 5 = 48,
  exactly), the USB PLL is set to 200 MHz for HSTX alone, and clk_peri
  takes the USB PLL halved, 100 MHz. clk_peri's divider is only two bits
  wide: asking for 240 / 5 silently gave 240 / 1, and SPI to the WiFi chip
  ran five times too fast.
- **Pads**: the HSTX pins get fast edges and 12 mA. Whether the defaults
  would do is not known.
- **Memory**: about 29 kB more SRAM than 640x480 (wider scanlines, more
  cells, a frame table twice the size); the C heap is left with 18.9 kB.

`vidstat -s` still dumps 80 x 30 cells: the system call copies 80 bytes a
row.
